1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember information about your visit.

2. Cookies On This Site

orbitpixelstudio.in is built as a lightweight static site. We don't currently set advertising or tracking cookies of our own. We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall traffic patterns.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some elements load from third parties — for example Google Fonts, and, where embedded, WhatsApp. These services may set their own technical cookies or collect data under their own privacy policies, independent of this site.

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking cookies may affect how some parts of third-party embedded content behave, though this site's core pages will still work.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

6. Contact Us

Questions about cookies can be sent via WhatsApp, call, or email at hello@orbitpixelstudio.in.
